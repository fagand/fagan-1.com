The Story

One site, every era.

From a free .TK domain in 2006 to the layout that closed it out — fagan-1.com was rebuilt, re-skinned and reborn more times than anyone kept count of. Scroll through the lot.

2006
2006 fagan.tk · where it started

Awrite troops.

A free .tk domain, green crystal banner, a hit counter and a pics uploader. "Welcome tae the new look site." The whole thing ran on nerve and a forum.

2007
2007 fagan-1.com · its own domain

Onto the real thing.

Off the .tk and onto its own name. Glossy blue chrome, weekly tune drops, a radio stream and a proper forum. "This site should be updated every week wae new tunes."

'07
2007 new look 2007 · the graffiti era

Loud and hand-drawn.

Spray-can wordmark, cyan everywhere, megamixes uploaded most nights. Ballistic Beats, Supreme, Dj Blitzed — the update list never stopped.

'08
2007–08 pro ev 7 · darker & sharper

Going pro.

Black chrome and electric-blue veins. Layouts for sale, affiliates down the bottom, a donate button to keep the lights on. The site had a scene around it.

FIN
2008 the final layout

The last redesign.

Clean light-blue frame, CDs added, DJ tunes on tap — the layout that saw the site out before it went quiet. Everything you're standing in now is a homage to this.
