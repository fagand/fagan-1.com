Est. 2006

Welcome to Fagan-1.Com, a cherished piece of digital history! Between the years of 2006 and 2009, Fagan-1.Com was one of the most frequented online destinations for teenagers in Scotland, who were eagerly seeking the then-trending PCDJ music. My commitment to providing regular updates and a reliable platform contributed significantly to the site’s popularity during that time.

In the present day, the demand for this genre of music has waned, and the website has seen a decrease in visitors. However, I understand that there may still be individuals who stumble upon the site, perhaps those who fondly remember it from yesteryears.

For those of you who are not familiar with the website, I invite you to explore its history through the web archive by clicking the link here.
