Welcome to Fagan-1.Com, a cherished piece of digital history. Between 2006 and 2009, this was one of the most frequented online destinations for teenagers in Scotland seeking then-trending PCDJ music.

The demand has waned, but I understand there may still be those who stumble upon the site — perhaps fondly remembering it from yesteryears.
