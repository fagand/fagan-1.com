# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are mostly people who knew fagan-1.com as teenagers in Scotland between 2006–2009,
now in their thirties, stumbling back onto the site out of curiosity or nostalgia. Secondary
audience: anyone landing from David's socials (@iamfagan) or davidfagan.co.uk who wants a
quick sense of who he is. They arrive on phones as often as desktops, usually in a relaxed,
browsing mood — nobody is here to complete a task.

## Product Purpose

A living memorial to Scotland's original PCDJ site. It exists to honour the site's history,
serve the archived mixtapes, and act as a small personal playground for David to experiment
(e.g. the motivation video page). Success = a visitor smiles, plays a mixtape, and feels the
site was made with care.

## Brand Personality

Nostalgic, playful, personal. A loving homage to a 2006 Scottish PCDJ site — a personal time
capsule with humour and heart. The current visual voice is "deep-space HUD": black, neon-lime
(#ccff00), mono type, viewfinder ticks, starfield canvas. Warmth comes from the copy and the
music, precision from the chrome.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero metric, gradient text, identical card grids).
- Corporate portfolio templates — this is a personal site, not a CV.
- Sterile minimalism that strips out the site's history and humour.
- Heavy third-party widget soup (the old jQuery/Facebook/recaptcha era of the site).

## Design Principles

1. **The past is the product** — every page should acknowledge the site's 2006 lineage.
2. **One accent, used like a laser** — #ccff00 carries identity; everything else stays quiet.
3. **Fast homage** — nostalgia is no excuse for weight; pages must load instantly.
4. **Experiments are welcome, clearly marked** — new toys (motivation page) ship as
   experiments without destabilising the core pages.
5. **Made-by-a-person details** — small touches (est. badge, equalizer bars, shooting stars)
   over templated polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target declared; aim for WCAG AA pragmatics: never disable pinch-zoom,
respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (canvas + CSS), keep text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against black,
captions/controls for video where feasible. Motion is decorative everywhere — always provide
a reduced path.
